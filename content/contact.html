Parking and access

Parking is off the street at the side of the building. There is a ramp to the front door and the waiting room is on one level.

Making an appointment

Ring us during opening hours. If your animal is unwell today, ring in the morning and we will do our best to fit you in the same day.

New clients are welcome. Bring any history from your previous vet and we will take it from there.

After hours emergencies

Ring the Peninsula animal emergency centre in Mornington. Their number is on our answering machine.
