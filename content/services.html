Wattle Street Vet

Our Services

Consultations, vaccinations, surgery and dentistry for dogs, cats and rabbits, plus puppy preschool, prescription food and home visits. To book any of them, please ring us.

Ring us to book (03) 5550 1234

We do not take bookings by email.

Opening hours

Monday to Friday, 8.30am to 6.00pm

Closed weekends and public holidays. Surgery days are Tuesday and Thursday.

  • Consultations

    A standard consult runs for twenty minutes, which is longer than most clinics allow, because most problems take longer than ten minutes to explain.

    Twenty minutes, not ten

  • Vaccination and health checks

    Annual check-ups for dogs, cats and rabbits, with a full nose to tail examination and a written reminder when the next one is due.

    Dogs, cats and rabbits

  • Desexing and routine surgery

    Done on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Your animal comes in at 8am and goes home the same afternoon, and we ring you as soon as they are awake.

    Tuesdays and Thursdays, in at 8am

  • Dentistry

    Scale and polish, extractions, and honest advice about whether a tooth really needs to come out.

    Scale and polish, extractions

  • Puppy preschool

    Four Wednesday evenings, six puppies at a time, run by our nurse Bec. Bookings fill quickly, so ring early.

    Four Wednesday evenings, six puppies

  • Prescription food and flea treatment

    We stock the usual brands and will order anything we do not have on the shelf.

    The usual brands, and orders

  • Home visits

    For elderly animals and for end of life care, within ten minutes of the clinic. Please ring to arrange.

    Within ten minutes of the clinic

Finding us

We are at 42 Wattle Street, Mount Eliza. Parking is off the street at the side of the building, and there is a ramp to the front door.
